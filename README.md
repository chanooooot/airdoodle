# AirDoodle

Draw in air with a pinched thumb and index finger. Hold a closed fist for 0.6 seconds, or tap **Alive**, to animate the drawing.

Live: https://chanooooot.github.io/airdoodle/

Mobile-first. Uses your front camera locally for hand tracking; no account, server, or uploaded camera video.
